There are 8 course-slots (rated from A to H) in all the TAFs plus one 
optional track from April to May to be chosen between “Space technologies 
and applications” (Brest),  “Machine learning and applications” (Brest) or 
“Internet of Things” (Rennes)
Each course (UE) chosen by the student must be from a different course-
slot
   
To validate a TAF students must pass at least 7 courses (UE) 
out of 8 including:
 All the core courses (UE Coeur) of the TAF: A, B and C, which are 
mandatory 
 Elective courses (UE Electives) of the TAF 
 Free courses (UE libres):  students can take other UEs from the same 
course-slots in other TAFs

Embedded OS The goal of this UE is to discover the basics of embedded operating systems 
for the design of communicating objects. The module begins with a study of 
constrained devices to understand their hardware architecture and identify 
the various components. In a second step, different internal communication 
buses are studied such as UART, I2C and SPI. The third part studies the 
embedded operating system RIOT and allows to discover the hardware 
APIs. These concepts are applied to an IEEE 802.15.4 networks and finally 
the encryption aspects are implemented on the equipments.
